Editorial policy

How map pages are prepared and corrected

This policy explains how Wander Game Map organizes structured location data, publishes supporting text, handles automation, and responds to corrections.

Purpose and scope

Wander Game Map publishes interactive reference tools for video games. The primary editorial value is the combination of searchable marker data, category filters, clear labels, route-planning controls, and direct links between related maps. The site does not publish game reviews or claim that every marker is an official developer-provided location.

Structured data and normalization

Map pages are built from structured datasets containing areas, marker coordinates, categories, labels, descriptions, and media references. Automated generation is used to turn this data into consistent pages, but automation is not treated as a substitute for usefulness. The site adds navigation, searchable controls, category organization, marker totals, related maps, mobile behavior, and explanatory text around the raw data.

When labels are corrupted, unsupported, or not readable in English, the site may use a descriptive fallback based on the marker icon or category. It avoids presenting a guessed proper name as fact. Generic labels such as Point of Interest, Plant, Mineral, or Loot Container indicate that a more specific verified label was not available. Non-English marker descriptions are hidden behind an English fallback until they can be reviewed.

Guide text and generated summaries

Some supporting map summaries and FAQs are produced from verified fields in the current dataset, including the game name, map name, marker count, category names, available controls, and recorded update date. These summaries are constrained to what the page can demonstrate. They should not invent quest outcomes, item drop rates, combat strategies, or story details that are absent from the data.

Images, videos, and trademarks

Media is used to help identify a game, map, or location. Game artwork and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Wander Game Map is independent and is not affiliated with the listed game publishers or developers. Rights holders can request review or removal through the Contact page.

Corrections

Correction requests should include the exact URL, the marker or section involved, the current incorrect information, and the proposed correction. Reports are evaluated against the available dataset and visible page behavior. Clear factual corrections take priority over stylistic requests.

Advertising and editorial separation

Advertising does not determine which games are listed, how markers are labeled, or whether a correction is accepted. Sponsored placement is not currently offered. If that changes, commercial material will be identified clearly and kept separate from map data and editorial guidance.

The site does not ask visitors to click ads and does not place advertising in a way that imitates map markers, search results, filters, or navigation controls. See the Advertising Policy for more detail.
