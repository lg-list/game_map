Advertising policy

How advertising is handled on Wander Game Map

This page explains how advertising is separated from map content, how invalid traffic is avoided, and how visitors can control ad-related privacy settings.

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Editorial independence

Advertising does not influence which games are listed, which map areas are published, how marker categories are named, or whether a correction request is accepted. Map pages are built from structured game-location data and supporting page text is generated from visible dataset fields such as marker counts, map names, categories, and update dates.

Ad placement principles

Ads must not block navigation, cover map controls, imitate marker buttons, or ask visitors to click them. Interactive map controls, search results, filters, marker popups, and reset buttons are kept visually separate from advertising areas. The site does not place ads inside button groups or other controls where an accidental click would be likely.

Invalid traffic rules

Visitors should not click ads to support the site, test ads, refresh pages to generate impressions, or use automated tools to interact with ads. The site owner also avoids encouraging clicks, exchanging traffic, using misleading labels, or creating artificial ad activity. Suspicious behavior may be investigated through hosting, security, or advertising-provider signals.

Content suitability

Wander Game Map is intended as a practical reference for video game locations. Pages should use clear English, complete sentences, crawlable navigation, and working links. Unsupported or corrupted marker text is replaced with an English fallback instead of being presented as verified content. Pages that are incomplete, broken, or not useful should be corrected before they are promoted for advertising review.

Privacy and cookies

Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Region-specific consent controls may apply. More information is available in the Privacy Policy, including a link to Google's explanation of how it uses information from sites that use its services.

Reports

Report intrusive ads, misleading ad behavior, broken pages, rights concerns, or map content issues through the Contact page. Include the exact URL and a short description of what happened so the issue can be reviewed.
