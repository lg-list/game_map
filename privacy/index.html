Privacy policy

How data is handled on Wander Game Map

This policy describes the information that may be processed when you browse the site and the third-party services used to deliver pages, media, and advertising.

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Information you provide

Wander Game Map does not require a user account. If you contact the project through GitHub, the information you submit is handled according to GitHub's own privacy terms and may be publicly visible. Do not submit sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Technical and usage information

The hosting, content delivery, security, and advertising providers used by the site may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested URL, referrer, approximate region, timestamps, security signals, and cookie identifiers. This information may be used to deliver the site, prevent abuse, measure performance, and understand aggregate usage.

Cookies and advertising

Wander Game Map uses Google AdSense code on content pages. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, personalize, and measure ads, subject to the visitor's region and consent choices. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner sites policy.

The site does not ask visitors to click ads and keeps advertising separate from map controls, marker buttons, search results, and navigation. More detail is available in the Advertising Policy.

Where required, a consent message may be shown before advertising cookies are used. Browser settings can also be used to delete or block cookies, although doing so may affect some site features.

External content and links

Map pages may request tiles, images, or videos from external content providers. These providers receive the network information required to return the requested resource and may apply their own privacy policies. Links to third-party websites are provided for reference; Wander Game Map does not control their data practices.

Data retention

The site itself does not maintain personal profiles or account databases. Hosting, security, analytics, and advertising providers retain technical records according to their own retention schedules and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser, use available ad privacy controls, decline optional consent choices where shown, and avoid submitting personal information through public contact channels. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the site's hosting, advertising, contact, or measurement practices change. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Contact page.
