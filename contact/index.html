Open a GitHub issue

The fastest contact route is the public issue tracker. Do not include passwords, payment details, private account information, or other sensitive personal data.

Open issue tracker

What to include

  • The exact Wander Game Map page URL.
  • The game, area, category, or marker involved.
  • A concise description of what is incorrect or broken.
  • A source or screenshot when it helps verify the correction.

Rights and removal requests

Rights holders should identify the protected work, the page where it appears, their relationship to the work, and the requested action. Requests are reviewed before content is changed or removed.

Response expectations

Clear factual corrections and broken-site reports receive priority. The project does not guarantee a specific response time, but reports remain visible in the tracker until they are reviewed or closed.
